# Exercise content policy

- Only add real exercises supplied by the repository owner. Never generate demo exams, questions, choices, sample worked examples, answer keys, explanations, or fake results.
- Record correct answers and explanations ONLY when explicitly supplied by the owner. Never infer, solve, fill in, or invent missing answers, even when a question seems easy.
- If the owner supplies a question paper without an answer key, keep the key absent. Do not score it or label answers correct/incorrect until its answer key is supplied.
- Preserve the independent app access and parent passwords. Answer keys, explanations and scores remain parent-password protected.
- Keep an empty catalog when no real papers have been supplied. Do not restore the removed illustrative material.
- When the owner sends a topic and worked example, update the application immediately. The first supplied topic is TIMO Grade 2: Bài toán về tuổi (Age problem), Amy/John example, with the owner's two supplied solutions. Worked teaching examples are distinct from practice/exam answer keys.
- Classify incoming exam questions into owner-supplied topics using their examples. Put unmatched or uncertain questions in "Chưa có chủ đề"; do not invent additional topic names.
- The second supplied TIMO Grade 2 topic is Bài toán về ngày tháng (Date Problem). It contains the owner's two examples: Wednesday/day-after-tomorrow with 17 days later (Thursday), and 17 February 2020 to 27 April 2020 (Monday). Use only the solutions supplied in those images.
- The third supplied TIMO Grade 2 topic is Dạng bài 1 (Problem type 1): 36 chickens/rabbits, 100 legs. Preserve the supplied bilingual assumption-method solution and supplied answer (22 chickens, 14 rabbits). Do not invent additional examples or solutions.
- Topic 3 also contains Example 2, labeled "Problem type 2" in the owner's image: chickens exceed rabbits by 14, total 172 legs. Preserve the supplied bilingual algebra solution and supplied answer (24 rabbits). This is a second example within topic 3, not a fourth topic.
- When a supplied question or solution includes figures, reproduce them as closely as possible: preserve arrangement, animal/object counts, labels, colors and mathematical meaning. Never omit an instructional figure or invent its mathematical content. Topic 3 Example 1 includes the owner's oval chicken/rabbit diagrams and the solution's downward arrow labeled −4; keep that annotation as supplied rather than silently changing it.
- Topic card badges must automatically number topics 1, 2, 3, ... in catalog order, independently of numeric words in their names or IDs (e.g. "Problem type 1" is topic 3).
